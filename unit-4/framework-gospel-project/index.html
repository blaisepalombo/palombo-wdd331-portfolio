Parables of Jesus

Stories from the New Testament that teach faith, mercy, forgiveness, and love.

Key Parables

A few parables that show how Jesus taught through simple stories with deeper meaning.

Compassion

The Good Samaritan

Jesus teaches that loving your neighbor means helping anyone in need, even if they are different from you.

Luke 10:25–37

Forgiveness

The Prodigal Son

A son wastes his inheritance but returns home. His father welcomes him with love, showing God’s mercy and forgiveness.

Luke 15:11–32

Worth of Souls

The Lost Sheep

Jesus compares God’s love to a shepherd who leaves ninety-nine sheep to search for the one that is lost.

Luke 15:4–7

Faith

The Mustard Seed

Faith can begin small like a mustard seed but grow into something strong and lasting.

Matthew 13:31–32

Why Jesus Taught in Parables

These stories made gospel principles easier to understand and apply.

Jesus often used familiar situations so people could understand spiritual lessons through everyday life.

Parables invited people to think more deeply about repentance, faith, forgiveness, humility, and the kingdom of God.

Many parables encouraged listeners to look at their own hearts and consider how they could follow Christ more faithfully.
